**PADER Safety Report**

### 1. Executive Summary
This PADER safety report summarizes the safety data for a marketed product over a one-year reporting period, from December 27, 2024, to December 26, 2025. The report includes an analysis of 1024 cases, with 1023 serious cases and 1 non-serious case.

### 2. Case Summary
A total of 1024 cases were reported during the one-year reporting period. The cases were distributed across various countries, with the top 5 countries being EU (333), United Kingdom (278), France (186), Canada (55), and Italy (52).

### 3. Serious Case Summary
There were 1023 serious cases reported, accounting for 99.9% of all cases. The serious cases included 68 fatal cases and 955 non-fatal cases. The most frequently reported serious reactions were Acute kidney injury (80), Drug ineffective (53), Hypotension (46), and Drug interaction (43).

### 4. Demographic Summary
The demographic characteristics of the patients who reported adverse events were:
- Sex: Female (503), Male (493), Unknown (28)
- Age: Mean (70.07), Median (73.0), Minimum (1.0), Maximum (104.0)
- Age groups: 76+ (380), 61-75 (363), 46-60 (134), Unknown (84), 31-45 (36), <18 (16), 18-30 (11)

### 5. Adverse Event Summary
The top 5 most frequently reported adverse events were:
- Acute kidney injury (80)
- Drug ineffective (54)
- Hypotension (46)
- Drug interaction (43)
- Dyspnoea (38)

### 6. Outcome Summary
The outcomes of the reported cases were:
- Recovered/Resolved (494)
- Unknown (330)
- Recovering/Resolving (227)
- Not recovered/Not resolved/Ongoing (195)
- Fatal (68)
- Recovered/Resolved with sequelae (21)

### 7. Safety Findings
The safety findings of this report indicate that the product is associated with a high number of serious adverse events, including acute kidney injury, drug ineffective, hypotension, and drug interaction. The report also highlights the importance of monitoring patient outcomes and reporting adverse events.

### 8. PADER Guidance
According to the PADER guidance, this report should include a narrative summary and analysis, a history of actions taken, and a case index/listing. However, since no action information was provided, this report explicitly states that no actions were reported.

### 9. Conclusion
In conclusion, this PADER safety report highlights the importance of monitoring adverse events and patient outcomes. The report identifies the most frequently reported adverse events and serious reactions, and provides a demographic summary of the patients who reported adverse events. The findings of this report will inform future safety monitoring and risk management strategies for the product.
